Personal, experimental project

personal-health is an independent personal project maintained by michael-bill. Its purpose is to provide read-only access to the account owner's Fitbit history through the Google Health API. Development is at an early stage. The public website is informational and does not provide a hosted health-data service.

Authorized use

Use the connection only with an account you own or are explicitly authorized to access. Google authorization is required. You are responsible for configuring your installation, protecting credentials and stored records, and complying with applicable Google and Fitbit service terms.

Connected assistants and services

If you configure an MCP client or AI provider, you are responsible for deciding what information to send and reviewing that service's terms and privacy settings. The privacy policy explains the intended data categories and handling.

Health information

Device measurements and generated interpretations may be incomplete or inaccurate. This project is not a medical device, emergency service or substitute for professional diagnosis or treatment. Do not rely on it for urgent or consequential medical decisions.

Availability and changes

Features may change during development. Data availability depends on Google, Fitbit, the device and the account's granted permissions. The project makes no promise of uninterrupted service, completeness or accuracy. You may stop using it and revoke access at any time.

Contact

For project questions, use GitHub issues. Do not post private health data or secrets there.
